About the role

Riverbend's lending team needs an analyst to keep its consumer credit models honest. You will track portfolio performance, explain changes to loan officers in plain language and help set lending limits each quarter.

Each month you will report on delinquency and loss rates, test scoring rules against last year's applications and present your findings to the credit committee.

Requirements

  • SQL and one of Python or R
  • Clear writing for readers who are not analysts
