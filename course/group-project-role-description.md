# Group Project – Role Description

> Source: [Canvas – Group Project - Role Description](https://baylor.instructure.com/courses/257917/pages/group-project-role-description) (CSI 5324, Fall 2026)

## Group Activities

Students are expected to work together to meet specified deadlines and overall group objectives. The minimum prescribed roles in each group are:

1. Project Manager
2. Requirements Engineer
3. Design Engineer
4. Quality Assurance Engineer
5. Project Librarian

> **Note:** Each student is expected to participate in all activities even though they are assigned to be in charge of one (or two) ownership role(s). Assigned role(s) can be changed if needed.

## Project Role Descriptions

- **Project Manager** — responsible for coordinating the activities of the group and for liaison with the instructor to resolve issues affecting the group. Should be professional and responsible, a good organizer, and an effective communicator. Functions:
  1. Coordinating group activities
  2. Overseeing and managing activities
  3. Allocating roles, activities, and responsibilities
  4. Coordinating and managing documentation activities
- **Requirements Engineer** — leads the requirements effort.
- **Design Engineer** — leads the design effort.
- **Quality Assurance Engineer** — oversees test case design, validation of requirements, design, etc.
- **Project Librarian** — keeps all meeting logs and makes all design artifacts available for the team.

> **Note:** Every member of the group should work as a developer/tester for at least three use cases.
